AL 📍 Stockholm, SE

Designer who codes. Developer who sweats the details.

I've spent the last decade moving between design and engineering — which means I speak both languages fluently. I care about the 0.1s of latency you feel but can't explain, the focus ring nobody notices until it's gone, and the copy that makes a confusing screen obvious.

These days I partner with small teams to ship their first version, and with bigger ones to untangle their design systems. Either way, I like being close to the code and close to the customer.

Away from the screen: long-distance running, film photography and an ongoing battle with sourdough.
